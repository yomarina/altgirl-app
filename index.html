Before you sign up

How do I get in?

With a code from a friend or a local guide. No code? Join the beta list below: it’s free, and you get the invite first.

What can she do today?

In the Da Nang alpha: Grab rides and booking a training at your club. New scenarios ship every week; anything not live yet is marked “in build”.

How does the beta work?

It opens on iPhone and Android at the same time. When it does, we email you an invite: on iPhone it comes through TestFlight, Apple’s app for beta versions; on Android, through Google Play. We won’t promise a date we can’t keep.

What happens to my data?

Your Altgirl is separate from everyone else’s. What she learns is kept on your phone where possible. We don’t sell it.

Is the card a bank card?

It will be issued by licensed partners; YO:Labs is not a bank. Pricing, networks and countries come before launch.
